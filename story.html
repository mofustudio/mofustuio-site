Our story

We grew up inside the loom's reach.

Sanchit, Ekansh and Himanshu were born and raised in Agra (the city of Taj), a childhood spent within a few hours of nearly every great weaving town in India.

From Agra, the craft surrounds you. Drive a little in any direction and you arrive at a loom that has been running for generations. We watched wool become knots, and knots become rugs, long before we knew it would become our work.

Bhadohi and Mirzapur, a stretch to the east, are the heart of India's hand-knotted trade. Jaipur, to the west, carries centuries of colour and pattern. Panipat, to the north, is where much of the country's woven flooring is finished. And far to the north, Kashmir gives us the fine silk-and-wool knots that take a weaver months to complete.

Today the three of us live on three continents. What we share is the same conviction we grew up with: that a rug made by hand, by people who have done it their whole lives, is one of the finest objects a home can hold. Mofu Studio exists to put those rugs in front of the buyers who value them most.
